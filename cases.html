Clinical researchPredictive modelling

Optimal timing of anticoagulation in traumatic brain injury

Clinicians needed to know when it is safe to start anticoagulation after traumatic brain injury, balancing bleeding risk against clots, with only weak existing evidence.

Approach

We drew on National Trauma Data Bank data, used propensity-score modelling with gradient boosting, Cox proportional-hazards models and restricted cubic splines, and built a Shiny app for bedside risk prediction.

We found a non-linear timing effect, with very early treatment more favourable, and gave clinicians a tool that supports a clearer window for starting treatment.
