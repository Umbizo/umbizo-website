Historical DNA analysis for Native American ancestry verification

A genetic genealogy project used Y-chromosome STR analysis and historical records to test a possible father-son relationship between a Scottish historical figure and a Cherokee tribal member, where original documentation was lost in the Civil War era.

The challenge

A complex genealogical investigation needed DNA evidence to test a potential father-son relationship between a Scottish historical figure and a Cherokee tribal member. Traditional documentation had been lost during the Civil War era, and family history carries particular importance in Native American cultures, making a robust genetic approach essential.

What we set out to do

  • Analyse Y-chromosome DNA profiles across family lines
  • Evaluate historical documentation for contextual support
  • Quantify relationship likelihood through STR analysis
  • Compare genetic variation patterns between families
  • Provide conclusive evidence of paternal relationships

Our approach

DNA profile analysis

Collected Y-chromosome DNA samples, examined 30 Y-STR markers, identified genetic variations, and mapped generational distances.

Historical documentation review

Assessed civil records, evaluated tribal documentation, analysed contextual evidence, and integrated historical timelines.

Statistical analysis

Calculated STR variation rates, determined generational distance, assessed relationship probabilities, and validated findings across samples.

Results and impact

The analysis identified eight Y-STR differences across 30 markers, established a generational distance of roughly 370 years, and confirmed a 10-20 generation separation. The paternal lineages of seven individuals were verified. The work confirmed historical family connections, strengthened the tribal historical record, enhanced genealogical documentation, and preserved cultural heritage information.

“This work helped bridge a gap in Native American family history that traditional documentation could not fill.”

Michael Brown, Lead Genetic Genealogist

Timeline

  • Sample collection: 2 months
  • DNA analysis: 3 months
  • Historical research: 2 months
  • Final analysis and report: 1 month

Looking forward

Expanding the methodology to other Native American families and developing more comprehensive ways of combining genetic evidence with historical documentation for ancestry verification.
