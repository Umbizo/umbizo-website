Mortality reduction with implanted defibrillator for primary prevention of sudden death after myocardial infarction: temporal trends in the PROFID study

Using pooled data from more than 32,000 heart attack survivors with reduced heart function, the study found that the survival benefit of a preventive implanted defibrillator has narrowed over the past three decades. It comes from the PROFID programme, where Umbizo delivered nine analyses of sudden cardiac death after heart attack.
